The Blues Driver is a fine pedal being quietly rude to bass players: its
input stage rolls off low end early enough that a four-string comes out
thinner than it went in. Two capacitor swaps move that roll-off down where
it does no harm. While the pedal is open, the same evening will convert
the electronic bypass to a mechanical true bypass — two mods, one
disassembly.

Both changes are documented separately in the guide, and you can do
either without the other. The bass-lite variant splits the difference
for guitarists in drop tunings.
