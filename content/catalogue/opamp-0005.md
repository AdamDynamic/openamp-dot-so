The SD-1's whole personality is a small act of unfairness: two diodes clip
one half of the waveform, one clips the other. That asymmetry is where the
warmth lives — and adding a third diode on a toggle lets you even the odds
and hear the pedal go from tube-ish shove to tighter, TS-style compression
at the flick of a switch.

This is the simplest mod in the catalogue: one diode, one toggle, three
wires, and the courage to drill a Boss enclosure. The guide includes a
drilling template. Use it — the only irreversible step in the whole job is
the one the drill makes.

The mod guide covers layout options for left- and right-handed
boards, and where to hide the wire run so the toggle looks factory.
