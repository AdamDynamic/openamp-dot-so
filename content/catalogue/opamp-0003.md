Boss service manuals are good news wearing a stern expression: every
compact pedal since 1978 is drawn the same way, so learn to read one and
you have learnt to read them all. The input buffer is always bottom left.
The flip-flop bypass is always the strange bit in the middle that everyone
skips. The interesting part — the bit that makes a DS-1 a DS-1 and not an
SD-1 — is nearly always one op-amp and a handful of parts you could cover
with a stamp.

This paper walks through one manual (the DS-1's) block by block, in plain
language, and ends with a one-page map you can lay next to any other Boss
schematic to get your bearings. Read it before attempting OPAMP-0004 and
the mod will make sense rather than merely work.
