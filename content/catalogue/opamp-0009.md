Three letters arrived this quarter, and two of them changed the files.

Priya wrote that the SD-1 drilling template saved her pedal but the
6.5 mm bit wandered off her mark anyway, and that the answer is to
centre-punch twice. She's right, and the guide now says so — that's
[v1.2 in the provenance](/catalogue/opamp-0005/), with her name on it. hexbender sent a redraw
of the Wasp panel on his own 25 mm grid, drawing enclosed, use it if
it's useful. It was; it ships with the next [Wasp](/catalogue/opamp-0001/) revision, credited. And
Colm found № 14 of the C60 in a Leeds record shop and asked who he
should return it to. Nobody. Dub it and pass it forward — that's the
licence working exactly as written, and № 14's travels are now in [the
register](/catalogue/opamp-0008/).

The C60 went out with this update: 38 of the 50 numbered copies have
gone. On the bench for October: a spring reverb tank in Kosmo format,
and the first pedal that isn't mine since [el_farad's tremolo fix](/catalogue/opamp-0007/).

For the record: letters 1–5 went out by post, before letters were
listed; № 6 is the first to take a catalogue number.

Write in — post@opamp.so. Letters are answered by return of post, or
here, when the answer is useful to everyone.
