Every output of the Society receives a sequential catalogue number,
allocated at shipping — never reserved, never skipped, never reused.
Entries are never retired. The catalogue grows; it doesn't churn.
Every buildable entry keeps its files — schematic, board layouts, BOM,
build guide — in its own repository, linked from its row and its page:
the catalogue points, the repository is the source of truth.
