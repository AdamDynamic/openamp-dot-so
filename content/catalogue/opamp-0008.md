Ten tracks by eight contributors, every one made on analogue or DIY
equipment — Kosmo systems with catalogue modules in them, modified Boss
pedals, a borrowed four-track with a sticking pinch roller that became a
feature. No computers were harmed, or indeed involved, past the point
of design.

## The programme

Side A — the loud material: *Clipping Point* (Jo M.), *Four of
Something* (Tomasz W.), *Wasp in the Room* (hexbender), *Drop Tuning*
(el_farad), *Battery Bay* (Ruth & Ash). Side B — the patient: *Slow
Blink* (Marta), *Braid* (Priya), *Second Life* (el_farad), *Unity*
(Adam), *Passing Forward* (everyone who was still in the room).

Every piece was made on catalogue equipment or the machines it was
built to sit beside. The J-card credits each contributor the way they
asked to be credited.

There is no download. The C60 is the release: side A for the loud
material, side B for the patient. If that seems precious, borrow a copy
from whoever has one and dub it — that isn't piracy here, it's
distribution.

## The register

Numbered copies whose owners have written in, and where they are now:
№ 03 — Sheffield. № 14 — Leeds, by way of two dubs. № 23 — with the
correspondent (pictured). № 31 — Rotterdam. № 44 — Cardiff. Write to
borrow the nearest one; write again when yours moves on.
