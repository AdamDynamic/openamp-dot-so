## What this is

A growing catalogue of open-source projects for people who make
music with physical equipment — and the society of people who build
from it. Joining has no procedure; reading is enough. The first entries are modifications for guitar effects
pedals — primarily Boss — and modules in the Kosmo modular synth format.
Future entries will include original pedal and kit designs, recorded
releases on cassette, and events.

Everything is open by default for non-commercial use. The shop exists for
people who can't or don't want to build their own. The Society is the
community around it.

## Who it's for

People who make music with physical equipment: hobby builders who learn by
doing, and the people around them who want the same gear but don't (yet)
build it themselves. No prior expertise required, no professional engineers
expected. Curiosity is the only entry requirement.

## The Society

Self-selecting. There is no signup, no roster, no card, no gate.
Joining the Society is something you do by building a project, buying a
pedal, or wearing a t-shirt — not by filling in a form.

## Contributors

The Society has contributors, plural. Each project is attributed to a named
contributor — a member of the community — using whatever form of name they
choose. Adam is the current correspondent and first contributor, treated on
par with any other. The institution is the Society; the project is bigger
than anyone in it.

## The catalogue number

Every output — mod, module, kit, release, poster, shirt, letter — receives
a sequential catalogue number: OPAMP-0001, OPAMP-0002, and so on. Numbers
are allocated at shipping, never reserved, never skipped, never reused.
Entries are never retired. URLs are permanent.

## How it works

Schematics, layouts and build guides are free to copy and improve, for
non-commercial use; the shop sells finished pieces and kits, and pays
the bills — nothing more. Guides give enough to get started and trust
you with the rest. Revisions are kept and credited; nothing is retired.
Updates come quarterly, considered rather than constant. Cassettes,
hardware, paper: not because old is better, but because constraint
creates attention.

## Writing in

There is no form, and there never will be. Post a photograph and a line
about what you built to the correspondent — post@opamp.so — and it will
be considered for the builds gallery with the next quarterly update.
Letters are answered personally, by return of post — or in the
quarterly letter, itself a numbered entry in the catalogue, when the
answer is useful to everyone.
Designs, recordings and corrections travel the same road. How an entry
earns a number: it gets built, it gets documented, it ships. Marta's
Quad LFO and el_farad's tremolo fix earned theirs that way; yours
would too.

## Licences

Designs and documentation are published under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 — open,
non-commercial, share-alike. Recorded releases carry an analogue sharing
licence: copying and passing forward on physical analogue media is
permitted and encouraged; digital reproduction and upload are not. Copies
inherit the licence.
